---
title: "What's not there yet"
description: 'Know what ChapterJS does not do yet, how to recognise it, and what to do meanwhile'
---

ChapterJS is young. This page lists what it does not have yet, so that you do not look for it, and what to do meanwhile when there is something.

<Note>
  What exists is in the [reference](/reference): slash commands, events, components (buttons, select menus, forms, embeds), tasks and the structures they give you.
</Note>

## Autocomplete

Options whose suggestions come from your bot while the person types. Not available: an option takes `choices`, declared once in the file.

Meanwhile: `choices`, when the values are known in advance (25 at most).

## Context menu commands

Commands used by right-clicking a user or a message. Not available: a file of `src/commands/` is always a slash command.

Meanwhile: a slash command with a `user` option, or a `string` option that takes the link of a message.

## Presence and status

Showing "Playing..." or "Listening to..." under the name of the bot, or marking it idle. Not available.

## Other events

The [events](/reference/events) your files can react to are the ones listed there: messages, members, servers, channels and roles. Reactions, voice, typing, threads joined and left, bans, invites, scheduled events and the others are not there yet.

## A bot spread over several machines

A large bot runs on [several processes of one machine](/run/start#large-bots) by itself. Running it on several machines at once is not available.

## Choosing what the bot remembers

How many members, users and messages the bot keeps in memory is decided by the files of your project and by the memory available: see [What the bot remembers](/memory). It is not a setting you can change.
